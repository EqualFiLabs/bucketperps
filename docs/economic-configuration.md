# Economic configuration

Winning fees total 190 basis points of adjusted profit; loss fees total 200 basis points of the settled loss basis. The development split is 100/70/20 winning and 100/80/20 losing for stakers/protocol/Operators. An unfunded fee is waived in full. With zero effective staking weight, the staker slice stays in house cash and creates no retrospective entitlement.

The development configuration contains example numeric settings, including a flat loss-reward rate. Governor can change the rate directly for future settlements. Reward issuance consumes available unallocated STATICS inventory, records any shortfall and creates no IOU. Public wrapping and unwrapping start disabled with zero capacity. Paired trades, claim reuse and NFT resale still consume the same finite reward inventory. Off-protocol hedge costs and market impact are outside the synthetic book.

The production reward schedule, taper dates, inventory budget, market limits, keeper settings, surplus split and safety delays remain decisions for the operator. The contracts expose ordinary configurable parameters; there is no additional calibration switch. A published schedule should identify the initial raw reward units per dollar, each effective change time and next rate, funding budget and governing transaction references. No production schedule is asserted here.
